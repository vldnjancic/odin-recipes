6-Ingredient Garlic Butter Shrimp

Description

Zesty and quick to prepare, this 6-Ingredient Garlic Butter Shrimp is a weeknight lifesaver. Juicy shrimp bathed in a fragrant garlic butter sauce will have everyone asking for seconds.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb large, wild-caught shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter, cubed and chilled
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1/4 tsp crushed red pepper flakes
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Pat the shrimp dry with paper towels to ensure a good sear.
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat until hot, about 2 minutes.
  3. Add the shrimp in a single layer, cooking for 1 minute per side until just pink. Remove shrimp and set aside.
  4. Reduce heat to medium and add butter to the skillet. Tip: Let the butter melt slowly to prevent burning.
  5. Add minced garlic and red pepper flakes, sautéing for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  6. Return shrimp to the skillet, tossing to coat in the garlic butter sauce.
  7. Drizzle with fresh lemon juice and sprinkle with chopped parsley, stirring to combine.
  8. Cook for an additional 1 minute until shrimp are fully opaque and sauce is slightly thickened.
