Easy 6-Ingredient Chicken Stir Fry

Description

Fast, flavorful, and fuss-free, this chicken stir fry brings dinner to the table in minutes. Perfect for busy weeknights, it’s a crowd-pleaser with minimal prep.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breasts, sliced into thin strips
  • 2 tbsp vibrant sesame oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced to aromatic perfection
  • 1 cup crisp broccoli florets
  • 1/2 cup crunchy red bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp savory soy sauce

Steps

  1. Heat sesame oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering, about 1 minute.
  2. Add chicken strips, spreading them in a single layer. Cook undisturbed for 2 minutes to achieve a golden sear.
  3. Flip chicken pieces, then add garlic. Stir constantly for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Toss in broccoli and bell pepper. Stir fry for 3 minutes, until vegetables are bright and slightly tender.
  5. Pour soy sauce over the mixture. Stir to coat evenly, cooking for another minute to meld flavors.
  6. Remove from heat immediately to prevent overcooking.
