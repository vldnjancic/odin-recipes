6-Ingredient One-Pot Chili

Description

Nothing beats a hearty chili that comes together with minimal effort and maximum flavor. This 6-ingredient one-pot chili is your weeknight savior, packed with bold tastes and ready in no time.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef, preferably 80/20 for richness
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced for sweetness
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced for a pungent kick
  • 1 can (15 oz) kidney beans, drained and rinsed for texture
  • 1 can (15 oz) diced tomatoes, with juices for acidity
  • 2 tbsp chili powder, for deep, smoky heat

Steps

  1. Heat a large pot over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef, breaking it apart with a spoon. Cook until no pink remains, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add the diced onion and minced garlic to the pot. Stir frequently until the onion is translucent, about 3 minutes.
  3. Tip: For deeper flavor, let the onions and garlic slightly caramelize.
  4. Stir in the chili powder, coating the beef and onions evenly. Cook for 1 minute to toast the spices.
  5. Add the diced tomatoes with their juices and the drained kidney beans. Bring to a simmer.
  6. Tip: For a thicker chili, let it simmer uncovered. For more liquid, cover the pot.
  7. Reduce heat to low. Simmer for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally to prevent sticking.
  8. Tip: Taste and adjust seasoning with salt if needed, but the chili powder usually provides enough flavor.
  9. Remove from heat. Let stand for 5 minutes to thicken slightly before serving.
